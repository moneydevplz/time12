How it's built

Plain web technology, chosen to stay quick on school laptops and phones.

Vanilla JavaScript

No front-end framework. The dashboard is a single page of ES modules, so there is nothing heavy to download or hydrate.

Supabase

Postgres and Edge Functions power sign-in, organizations, tasks, notes sync and file upload and download.

IndexedDB

Notes live in your browser first, with a sync queue and an automatic sync every five seconds.

SheetJS

Reads .xlsx, .xls and .csv imports and writes your Excel exports, all inside the browser.

WebGL

A shader draws the soft aurora behind sign-in and the app, with glass panels over it. Lower End mode switches it off.

DM Serif Display and DM Sans

One serif for titles and one sans for everything else, in light and dark themes you can toggle with Ctrl J.

Tuned to your device

On your first visit, Time12 asks what you are using. It also reads reduced-motion and data-saver settings and drops to a lighter mode on its own if frames run slow. Switch any time from the button beside the theme toggle.

Common questions

Who creates tasks, groups and accounts?

The organization account, meaning whoever registers. It adds groups, sub-groups and member accounts. Member accounts do not see the Add button in the Org tab.

What can a member hand in?

Text, links and files, in any combination. Files can be up to 25 MB each. At least one of the three is required.

What happens when a submission is rejected?

The task returns to the member as Rejected and they can submit again until the deadline. In Analytics it shows as Redo, and the task view says it needs resubmission.

What happens after the deadline?

A task nobody handed in is marked overdue, and the member sees Deadline Passed — Submissions Closed. Analytics counts it as missed.

Can I export results?

Yes. The Analytics tab has a Download Excel button for the current view: the whole organization, a group, a person or a single task.

Do I need an email address to sign up?

No. You register with a username and a password. Accounts you create for members use the same, named under your organization.

Does it work offline?

Notes do. They are saved on your device first and sync when you are back online. Tasks and submissions need a connection.
