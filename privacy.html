Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Time12 ("we", "our", or "us") operates https://time12.xin and the Time12 MCP connector for AI assistants such as ChatGPT.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your username and authentication identifiers when you sign in to Time12.
  • Workspace Content: Tasks, notes, files, organization memberships, and productivity analytics you create or access through Time12.
  • OAuth Access Tokens: Scoped, revocable tokens issued when you authorize an AI client (such as ChatGPT) to connect to your Time12 account.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to provide, sync, and display your tasks, notes, and analytics inside Time12 and through authorized MCP tool calls requested by you. We do not sell your personal data or use your workspace content to train third-party AI models.

3. Third-Party Services

Time12 uses Supabase for database and authentication infrastructure and Cloudflare for hosting and content delivery. When you connect an external AI client via OAuth, data is shared with that client only within the scopes you explicitly approve (read, analytics, tasks, or files).

4. Data Retention & Revocation

You can revoke AI access at any time in Time12 Settings → AI connect (MCP) → Connected apps, which immediately invalidates the access token. You may also delete your notes, tasks, or account data directly within the app.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at support@time12.xin
